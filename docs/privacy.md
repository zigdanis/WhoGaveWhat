# WhoGaveWhat privacy policy

The published policy is at <https://ziganshin.pro/whogavewhat/privacy-policy>. Its source
is `whogavewhat/privacy-policy.html` in
[zigdanis/zigdanis.github.io](https://github.com/zigdanis/zigdanis.github.io), and the app
links to it from **Settings → Privacy Policy**.

When the app's data handling changes, update that page and its date in the same release,
and keep these in step with it:

- `WhoGaveWhat/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`
- the App Store privacy label: Other User Content and Device ID, not linked to the user, not
  used for tracking, for App Functionality
- the cloud icon consent text in the Add Gift sheet and Settings
- `icon-worker/README.md` for what the server stores and how long it keeps it
