• Your gifts are now safe in iCloud. The backup runs on its own, so a new iPhone or a reinstall won't lose a thing, and after a reinstall Home offers to bring everything back. You can turn it off in Settings → Backup.
• Settings → Backup can also export all your data to one file and import it back. Restoring and importing only add what's missing and never delete anything.
• Every gift now has its own currency: pick it next to the amount and set Default currency in Settings. Totals on Home, People and Insights are split by currency, so dollars and rubles never get mixed up.
• A person's screen has an Add a gift button, with that person already filled in.
• A new button swaps From and To in one tap.
• Tapped Save too fast? The gift icon still shows up a moment later.
• If something can't be saved or loaded, the app tells you and lets you try again. What you typed stays put.
• In Russian, the app on your Home Screen is now called «Кто Чё».
