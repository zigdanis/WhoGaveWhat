Who Gave What is a simple, fast way to keep track of the gifts you and your family give and receive. There's no way to hold it all in your head, and sooner or later you'll want to remember what you gave someone a year or two ago.

Now you're the one with the best memory in the family 😉

LOGGED IN SECONDS
• Everything is stored right on your iPhone, so saving is instant.
• Just enter the gift, who gave it and who received it. The app suggests an icon and an approximate price for you.

A HISTORY WITH EVERYONE
• Every person has their own gift history, so you can see at a glance who gave what and when.
• It's all clear right away: how much you've given and how much you've received in return.
• Compare any two people for any year.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account needed. Your people, gifts and amounts stay on your iPhone.
• No ads and no tracking.
• Cloud suggestions work only with your permission. People, dates and amounts are never sent.

MADE FOR iPHONE
• Liquid Glass design and support for both displays on iPhone Duo.
