Who Gave What is a warm little ledger for every gift you give and get.

Remember that Aunt Maria sent a teddy bear for New Year, that you brought wine to Igor's birthday, and who actually paid for the family present. No more guessing when it's your turn to return the favor.

LOGGED IN SECONDS
• Type what the gift was and the app picks a matching icon.
• Choose who gave it and who received it. The people and occasions you used most recently come first.
• Add the occasion and date. Add an approximate value only when it's useful.
• Mark gifts you paid for on someone else's behalf, so you can see who is really paying.

IT ADDS UP PER PERSON
• Every gift is linked to a person, with their photo and full gift history.
• See all your gifts in one timeline, month by month.
• Mark yourself in People to see your balance with each person: what you've received and what you've given back.
• Compare any two people side by side for any year.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account needed. Your people, gifts and amounts stay on your iPhone.
• No ads and no tracking.
• Optional cloud suggestions stay off until you allow them. When they're on, only the gift name, your currency and a random ID used to limit requests are sent, to choose an icon and suggest an approximate price. People, dates, amounts and occasions are never sent.

MADE FOR iPHONE
• A clean Liquid Glass design in light and dark mode.
• Uses both displays on iPhone Duo.
• In English and Russian, with values in US dollars or rubles.
